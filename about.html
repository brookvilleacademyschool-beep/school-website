Our school

A welcoming community with high expectations

Westbrook Secondary School provides a broad, ambitious education for students aged 11 to 16. We believe students thrive when they feel known, challenged and supported, and when they understand how their learning prepares them for adult life.

Curiosity

We develop deep knowledge and the confidence to ask thoughtful questions.

Community

Respect, kindness and responsibility shape our shared school culture.

Ambition

Every student is encouraged to set high goals and work persistently towards them.

Leadership and governance

Our senior leadership team works with teachers, families and governors to provide strong academic standards, excellent pastoral care and safe, purposeful facilities.
