Joining Westbrook

Westbrook welcomes applications from families seeking a place within Year 7 or another year group. Places are offered according to the published admissions policy and local authority procedures.

Starting in Year 7

Applications for Year 7 places should be made through the local authority admissions service. Our transition programme helps students and families prepare for the move from primary school.
