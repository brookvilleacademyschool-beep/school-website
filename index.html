Welcome to Westbrook

At Westbrook, every student matters.

We bring ambitious learning and genuine care together. Our students are encouraged to think independently, work collaboratively, and contribute with confidence.

From the classroom to the stage, playing field and community, Westbrook gives young people the support and challenge they need to thrive.
