Teaching and learning

A broad and ambitious education

Our curriculum gives students powerful knowledge, essential literacy and numeracy, and opportunities to apply learning across subjects. Teachers plan explicitly, check understanding carefully and provide regular feedback.

English & humanities

Literature, language, history, geography, citizenship and ethics.

Science & mathematics

Conceptual knowledge, investigation, reasoning and problem solving.

Arts & technology

Creative design, computing, performing arts, sport and practical learning.

Key Stage 3

Years 7 to 9 establish strong foundations in core subjects while offering a broad range of creative, technical and cultural experiences.

Key Stage 4

Years 10 and 11 provide a rigorous programme of GCSE subjects, independent study, careers guidance and preparation for post-16 education.

Learning support

Our SENCO and inclusion team work with students, families and teachers to remove barriers, provide targeted support and ensure fair access to the curriculum.
