A broad, ambitious curriculum

Our curriculum is designed to build deep knowledge, practical skills and confidence over time. Students study a broad range of subjects while receiving guidance to discover their strengths.

English & humanities

Literacy, history, geography, languages and culture.

Science & mathematics

Conceptual understanding, inquiry and problem solving.

Creative & technical

Arts, design, computing, technology and performance.

Key Stage 3

Students in Years 7 to 9 follow a carefully sequenced programme covering core, creative and vocational subjects, with strong foundations in literacy and numeracy.

Key Stage 4

Students in Years 10 and 11 receive subject choices and careers guidance alongside preparation for GCSE examinations and their next steps after school.

Learning support

Our inclusion team works with teachers and families to make teaching accessible to students with additional needs.
