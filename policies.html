Safeguarding

Safeguarding and promoting the welfare of every student is our highest priority. Concerns should be raised with the designated safeguarding team.

Our behaviour policy sets clear expectations while helping students learn from mistakes and build respectful habits.

Regular attendance is essential for achievement and wellbeing. Families should contact the school office to report absences.

Privacy & data
